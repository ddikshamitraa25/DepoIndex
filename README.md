## 1. Problem Overview

In modern commercial and class action litigation, depositions routinely span hundreds of pages of complex, non-linear testimony. Attorneys require topical indexes to locate key witness admissions, assess expert qualifications, prepare cross-examinations, and draft summary judgment briefs.

Existing generic LLM indexing tools suffer from fatal flaws in legal contexts:
1. **Citation Hallucination:** Probabilistic models fabricate page and line numbers that do not exist or misalign with actual testimony.
2. **Monolithic Over-Merging:** Semantic embeddings merge disparate sections across dozens of pages simply because they share vocabulary (e.g. merging early settlement references with closing opinions).
3. **Micro-Topic Fragmentation:** Brief objections, colloquy, or 10-minute recesses frequently derail topic boundaries, producing uninformative micro-topics.
4. **Lack of Provenance:** Topical summaries cannot be audited back to exact transcript lines without extensive manual cross-checking.

**DepoIndex** solves this by implementing a **provenance-first architecture**. Every topic generated by the system is anchored to verified court reporter coordinates and canonical source IDs (`P<page>:L<line>`). The language model or segmenter is strictly forbidden from inventing citations.

## 3. Extraction & Provenance Strategy

### 3.1 Geometric Coordinate Extraction
Standard text scrapers discard line geometry, leading to severe speaker misclassification (e.g. mistaking the English indefinite article "A" for the court reporter's witness marker `A`). 

DepoIndex extracts word bounding boxes `(x0, y0, x1, y1, text)` via PyMuPDF:
- **`Q` & `A` Speaker Column:** Located strictly at `135.0 <= x0 <= 160.0` (specifically `x0 = 145.5 pt`).
- **Indented Body Text:** Begins at `x0 >= 176.8 pt`.
- **Timestamps:** Filtered at `x0 >= 490.0 pt`.
- **Line Numbers:** Extracted from the left margin (`x0 <= 95.0 pt`, values 1–25).
- **Page Footers:** Extracted from bottom margin (`y0 >= 700.0 pt`, e.g. `Page 7`).

### 3.2 Immutable Source Identifiers
Every canonical line receives an immutable key:
```
P<page>:L<line>  (e.g., P7:L12, P87:L25)
```
Cross-page spans are formatted as:
```
P<start_page>:L<start_line> - P<end_page>:L<end_line>  (e.g., P7:L1 - P9:L18)
```

### 3.3 Provenance Validation Engine
The `ProvenanceValidator` (`src/provenance/validator.py`) enforces:
1. **Location Existence:** `(start_page, start_line)` and `(end_page, end_line)` must exist in the canonical transcript index.
2. **Chronological Validity:** `(start_page, start_line) <= (end_page, end_line)`.
3. **Source ID Integrity:** Every ID in `source_ids` must map to a real record in the canonical index.
4. **Evidence Grounding:** Supporting evidence excerpts must match tokens present in the source text span.

## 4. Topic Segmentation & Recurrence Strategy

### 4.1 Chunking & Procedural Coalescing
- **Turns:** Consecutive lines spoken by the same speaker are grouped into a `Turn`.
- **QAUnits:** Questions and answers are combined into `QAUnit` structures. Examination headers (e.g. `BY MR. PURCELL:`) attach directly to the question.
- **Procedural Coalescing:** Objections (`"Objection. Form."`), reporter notices, and off-the-record breaks (e.g. recesses at P37:L10 and P76:L5) are tagged. Short interruptions (< 6 lines) are absorbed into the ongoing substantive topic chunk to prevent micro-fragmentation.

### 4.2 Handling Topic Recurrence vs. Monolithic Merging
In legal depositions, counsel frequently circles back to prior topics (e.g. returning to the **CFPB Settlement Agreement** at P61, P82, and P86). 
- **`MAX_PAGES_CONTINUE = 8`:** Prevents semantic similarity from creating runaway 20+ page topics.
- **`RECURRENCE_GAP_PAGES = 4`:** When a similar topic recurs after 4 or more pages of intervening testimony, the system generates a distinct new chronological topic, labels it with contextual specificity, and links the topic IDs via `recurrence_of` and `related_topic_ids`.

## 9. Web Application

DepoIndex includes a clean, responsive FastAPI application designed for litigation attorneys:
- **Topic Browser:** Filter topics by keyword, evidence, or source ID (e.g. `P24:L1`).
- **Procedural Filter:** Toggle purely procedural interruptions on or off.
- **Semantic Search:** Instant cosine-similarity search powered by scikit-learn TF-IDF vectorizer.
- **Exact Source Viewer:** Click any topic to view the exact transcript lines, complete with source IDs (`P7:L12`), speaker tags, and verbatim testimony.

## 12. Failure Analysis & Edge Cases

Documented in `validation/failure_analysis.md`, analyzing 4 real difficult cases:
1. **Case 1 (False 'A' Speaker Regex):** Fixed by enforcing coordinate column filtering at `x0 = 145.5 pt`.
2. **Case 2 (Interrupted Verbal Fragments):** Fixed by filtering leading dashes (`--`) and courtesy preambles to ensure informative topic titles.
3. **Case 3 (Topic Recurrence vs Monolithic Merging):** Fixed via `MAX_PAGES_CONTINUE = 8` and `RECURRENCE_GAP = 4` with `related_topic_ids` linking.
4. **Case 4 (Procedural Interruptions):** Fixed with procedural chunk coalescing for off-the-record breaks.
